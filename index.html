Client talking points

We already picked the light on the shore

You may hear that the Fed, which sets short-term interest rates, raised them, and stocks rose a bit in the last session.

The early read before the next open is a little softer, which is normal noise around a loud headline.

Picture a boat in a choppy inlet. The useful thing is not a new argument about the waves. It is the lighthouse you already picked. Your plan is that light. Headlines are the water. We steer by the light you chose when things were calm enough to think.

The plan was written for weeks when the water feels choppy. This is one of those weeks, or it is a quiet one. Either way, the light does not move.

You can say it this way: “When the news gets loud, we look at your plan, not at the waves. We are staying with it.” “We do not need a new plan because the news is loud. We stay with the one you already have.”
