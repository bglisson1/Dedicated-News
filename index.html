Client talking points

A loud headline, and the plan still comes first

The S&P 500 closed Friday at 7,743.41, up 39.28 points, or 0.51 percent, and the Dow, the Nasdaq, and the Russell 2000 finished higher too.

Headlines say the Federal Reserve raised interest rates, the rate that influences what people pay to borrow, and the headline calls it the first move in years.

Before the next open, S&P 500 futures were down 0.32 percent from the prior settle, a small move next to that headline.

A day like this is already inside a long-term plan, and the plan is what your client hired you to protect.

What to say to clients:

  • You may hear a big market headline before the open. Your plan was built for weeks like this, and we are staying with it.
  • We do not need a new strategy because of one decision. Staying invested is how the long-term plan does its job.

Keep the conversation on the plan, the time horizon, and the discipline of staying invested.
